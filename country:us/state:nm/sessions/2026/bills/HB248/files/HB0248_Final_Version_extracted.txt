1 AN ACT
2 RELATING TO GENERAL OBLIGATION BONDS; AUTHORIZING THE
3 ISSUANCE AND SALE OF CAPITAL PROJECTS GENERAL OBLIGATION
4 BONDS TO MAKE CAPITAL EXPENDITURES FOR STATEWIDE SENIOR
5 CENTERS, LIBRARIES AND HIGHER EDUCATION; PROVIDING FOR A
6 PROPERTY TAX LEVY FOR PAYMENT OF PRINCIPAL OF, INTEREST ON
7 AND COSTS RELATED TO THE BONDS; REQUIRING APPROVAL OF THE
8 REGISTERED VOTERS AT THE 2026 GENERAL ELECTION OF THE STATE;
9 DECLARING AN EMERGENCY.
10
11 BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF NEW MEXICO:
12 SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.--This act may be cited as the
13 "2026 Capital Projects General Obligation Bond Act".
14 SECTION 2. PURPOSE.--For the purpose of providing funds
15 for capital expenditures as authorized in the 2026 Capital
16 Projects General Obligation Bond Act, general obligation
17 indebtedness of the state is authorized for the purposes and
18 in the amounts set forth in Section 10 of that act.
19 SECTION 3. BOND TERMS.--
20 A. The state board of finance, except as limited
21 by the 2026 Capital Projects General Obligation Bond Act,
22 shall determine the terms, covenants and conditions of bonds
23 issued pursuant to that act, including:
24 (1) date or dates of issue, denominations
25 and maturities; HTRC/HB 248/ec
Page 1

1 (2) principal amounts;
2 (3) rate or rates of interest; and
3 (4) provisions for redemption, including
4 premiums, registration and refundability, whether the bonds
5 are issued in one or more series and other covenants relating
6 to the bonds and the issuance thereof.
7 B. The bonds shall be in such form as the state
8 board of finance determines with an appropriate series
9 designation and shall bear interest payable as set forth in
10 the resolution of the state board of finance.
11 C. Payment of the principal of the bonds shall
12 begin not more than two years after the date of their
13 issuance, and the bonds shall mature not later than eleven
14 years after the date of their issuance. Both principal and
15 interest shall be payable in lawful money of the United
16 States at the office of the paying agent within or without
17 the state as the state board of finance may direct.
18 D. The bonds shall be executed with the manual or
19 facsimile signature of the governor or the state treasurer,
20 and the seal or a facsimile of the seal of the state shall be
21 placed on each bond, except for any series of bonds issued in
22 book entry or similar form without the delivery of physical
23 securities.
24 E. The bonds shall be issued in accordance with
25 the provisions of the 2026 Capital Projects General HTRC/HB 248/ec
Page 2

1 Obligation Bond Act, the Supplemental Public Securities Act
2 and the Uniform Facsimile Signature of Public Officials Act
3 and may be issued in accordance with the Public Securities
4 Short-Term Interest Rate Act.
5 F. The full faith and credit of the state is
6 pledged for the prompt payment when due of the principal of
7 and interest on all bonds issued and sold pursuant to the
8 2026 Capital Projects General Obligation Bond Act.
9 SECTION 4. EXPENDITURES.--The proceeds from the sale of
10 the bonds shall be expended solely for providing money to be
11 distributed for the purposes and in amounts not to exceed the
12 amounts set forth in Section 10 of the 2026 Capital Projects
13 General Obligation Bond Act and to pay expenses incurred
14 under Section 6 of that act. Any proceeds from the sale of
15 the bonds that are not required for the purposes set forth in
16 Sections 6 and 10 of that act shall be used for the purpose
17 of paying the principal of and interest on the bonds.
18 SECTION 5. SALE.--The bonds authorized under the 2026
19 Capital Projects General Obligation Bond Act shall be sold by
20 the state board of finance at such time and in such manner
21 and amounts as the board may elect. The bonds may be sold at
22 private sale or at public sale, in either case at not less
23 than par plus accrued interest to the date of delivery. If
24 sold at public sale, the state board of finance shall publish
25 a notice of the time and place of sale in a newspaper of HTRC/HB 248/ec
Page 3

1 general circulation in the state and may also publish the
2 notice in a recognized financial journal outside the state.
3 The required publications shall be made once each week for
4 two consecutive weeks prior to the date fixed for the sale,
5 the last publication to be at least five days prior to the
6 date of the sale. The notice shall specify the amount,
7 denomination, maturity and description of the bonds to be
8 offered for sale and the place, date and hour at which the
9 sealed bids shall be received. At the time and place
10 specified in the notice, the state board of finance shall
11 open the bids in public and shall award the bonds to the
12 bidder or bidders offering the best price for the bonds. The
13 state board of finance may reject any or all bids and
14 readvertise and may waive any irregularity in a bid. Except
15 for the bid of the state, the successful bidder shall make a
16 deposit of two percent of the principal amount of the bonds
17 in a form acceptable to the state board of finance no later
18 than the close of business of the day the state board of
19 finance notifies the successful bidder that the bid has been
20 accepted. The state board of finance may also sell the bonds
21 or any part of the bonds to the state treasurer or state
22 investment officer. The state treasurer or state investment
23 officer is authorized to purchase any of the bonds for
24 investment. The bonds are legal investments for any person
25 or board charged with the investment of any public funds and HTRC/HB 248/ec

1 may be accepted as security for any deposit of public money.
2 SECTION 6. EXPENSES.--The expenses incurred by the
3 state board of finance in or relating to the preparation and
4 sale of the bonds shall be paid out of the proceeds from the
5 sale of the bonds, and all rebate, penalty, interest and
6 other obligations of the state relating to the bonds and bond
7 proceeds under the federal Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as
8 amended, shall be paid from earnings on bond proceeds or
9 other money of the state legally available for such payments.
10 SECTION 7. PROPERTY TAX LEVY.--To provide for the
11 payment of the principal of and interest on the bonds issued
12 and sold pursuant to the provisions of the 2026 Capital
13 Projects General Obligation Bond Act, there shall be and
14 there is hereby imposed and levied during each year in which
15 any of the bonds are outstanding an ad valorem tax on all
16 property in the state subject to property taxation for state
17 purposes sufficient to pay the interest as it becomes due on
18 the bonds, together with an amount sufficient to provide a
19 sinking fund to pay the principal of the bonds as it becomes
20 due, and, if permitted by law, ad valorem taxes may be
21 collected to pay administrative costs incident to the
22 collection of such taxes. The taxes shall be imposed,
23 levied, assessed and collected at the times and in the manner
24 that other property taxes for state purposes are imposed,
25 levied, assessed and collected. It is the duty of all tax HTRC/HB 248/ec
Page 5

1 officials and authorities to cause these taxes to be imposed,
2 levied, assessed and collected.
3 SECTION 8. TREASURER--DUTIES.--The state treasurer
4 shall keep separate accounts of all money collected pursuant
5 to the taxes imposed and levied pursuant to the provisions of
6 the 2026 Capital Projects General Obligation Bond Act and
7 shall use this money only for the purposes of paying the
8 principal of and interest on the bonds as they become due and
9 any expenses relating thereto.
10 SECTION 9. IRREPEALABLE CONTRACT--AUTHORITY FOR
11 ISSUANCE.--An owner of bonds issued pursuant to the
12 provisions of the 2026 Capital Projects General Obligation
13 Bond Act may, either at law or in equity, by suit, action or
14 mandamus, enforce and compel the performance of the duties
15 required by that act of any officer or entity mentioned in
16 that act. The provisions of that act constitute an
17 irrepealable contract with the owners of any of the bonds
18 issued pursuant to that act for the faithful performance of
19 which the full faith and credit of the state is pledged.
20 Without reference to any other act of the legislature, the
21 2026 Capital Projects General Obligation Bond Act is full
22 authority for the issuance and sale of the bonds authorized
23 in that act, and such bonds shall have all the qualities of
24 investment securities under the Uniform Commercial Code,
25 shall not be invalid for any irregularity or defect in the HTRC/HB 248/ec
Page 6

1 proceedings for the issuance and sale of the bonds and shall
2 be incontestable in the hands of bona fide purchasers or
3 holders thereof for value. All bonds issued under the
4 provisions of that act, and the interest thereon, are exempt
5 from taxation by the state and any subdivision or public body
6 thereof.
